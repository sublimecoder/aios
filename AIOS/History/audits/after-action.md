# After-action register

> Standing register of **procedure** findings, appended by [[after-action]] at the end of
> every [[aios-ingest]] run. Facts compound into the wiki; this is where the OS's own
> deficiencies compound. A row leaves `open` only as `applied` (with the commit) or
> `declined` (with the reason) — rows are never deleted.
>
> Mechanical drift is fixed in the run that finds it and logged here as `applied`.
> Everything judgment-shaped waits for your confirmation, then goes to [[skill-builder]].
