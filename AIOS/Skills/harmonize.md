# harmonize

**Purpose:** propagate a convention change across notes that share that convention (rules, formatting, headings, labels, structures).

**Steps:**
1. Confirm the convention and its new form.
2. Find notes that follow the old convention.
3. Show planned edits; apply on confirmation. Don't touch content meaning, only the convention.
4. Log a dated line in [[Log]].

**Dependencies:** none. **Output:** updated notes + report.
