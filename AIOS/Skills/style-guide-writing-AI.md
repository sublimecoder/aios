# style-guide-writing-AI

**Purpose:** pointer. The full tell list now lives in the portable skill
**`ai-tells`** (`skills/ai-tells/SKILL.md`), so it is reachable from every repo —
anywhere prose gets written — not only with the vault open. Distilled 2026-07-24 from [Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing).

Ten tells ordered by how often they bite (copulative dodge, negative parallelism,
significance inflation, participial fake-analysis, vague attribution, formula
conclusion, rule of three, synonym-churn, promotional register, formatting
habits), a tested two-pass grep block, and a place to name your own deliberate
keeps — the em dash in a signoff, curly quotes matching your corpus.

**Do not restate those rules here.** One home per meaning; a second copy inflates
its rank and drifts.

**Dependencies:** `ai-tells`. **Output:** reference only.
