# style-guide-writing-AI

**Purpose:** AI trope blacklist — language to avoid so outputs don't read as AI-flavored.

**Avoid:**
- Em dashes. Use commas, periods, or parentheses.
- "It's not just X, it's Y" constructions and "in today's fast-paced world" filler.
- "Delve", "tapestry", "testament to", "navigate the landscape", "elevate", "unlock", "robust".
- Hedging padding ("it's worth noting that", "in conclusion").
- Over-formatting: needless bold, headers on short content, bullet lists where a sentence works.

**Prefer:** direct, concise, casual — like texting a friend. Actionable over theoretical.

**Dependencies:** none. **Output:** reference only.
