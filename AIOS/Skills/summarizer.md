# summarizer

**Purpose:** summarize a conversation, meeting note, or transcript into a fixed structure.

**Steps:**
1. Read the source.
2. Produce sections: **Summary**, **Takeaways**, **Topics**, **Next Steps**, **Transcript** (linked or appended).
3. Write to a new note or append to the one I name. Link related vault notes.

**Dependencies:** none. **Output:** structured summary note.
